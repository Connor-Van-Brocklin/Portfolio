Experimental Storytelling · Course Project

C.L.O.S.A.T.

For this project, I created an eight-panel storyboard based on a memory from my trip to Japan. I focused on one night in Osaka when I went out alone near Dotonbori looking for something to do. After talking with a bartender at an Irish bar, I was told about a nearby karaoke bar and decided to find it. Once there, I met a stranger, joined his group, and ended up spending the night singing karaoke with people I had never met before.

I used the C.L.O.S.A.T. structure to organize the story. The character is myself as a traveler exploring an unfamiliar city, while Osaka at night serves as the location. Objects such as the bar, karaoke room, drinks, signs, and microphone help establish the setting and progression of the night. The situation comes from being alone in a foreign place and deciding whether to interact with strangers. The main action is choosing to approach people, join their group, and participate in karaoke. The underlying theme is connection and openness, showing how a small decision to talk to someone can turn an unfamiliar place into a memorable experience.

Each panel moves the story forward from being alone and searching for something to do, to meeting people and ending the night sharing an experience with them. The storyboard represents a moment from my trip where stepping outside of my comfort zone led to an unexpected sense of belonging.
